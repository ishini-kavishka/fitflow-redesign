# FitFlow Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 2026  
**Application:** FitFlow  
**Category:** Health & Fitness

## 1. Introduction

FitFlow respects the privacy of its users and is committed to protecting personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and the choices users have when using the FitFlow mobile application.

## 2. Information We May Collect

FitFlow may collect information required to provide personalized fitness features, including:

- Basic profile information such as name, age, and fitness preferences.
- Fitness goals and workout preferences.
- Workout activity and progress information.
- Nutrition and meal tracking information.
- Photos voluntarily uploaded for nutrition logging.
- Community posts and interactions.
- App usage and performance information.

## 3. How We Use Information

The collected information may be used to:

- Generate personalized AI-powered workout plans.
- Provide nutrition tracking features.
- Display workout and fitness progress.
- Improve the user experience and application performance.
- Enable community and social features.
- Provide relevant notifications and reminders.

## 4. AI-Powered Features

FitFlow may use user-provided fitness goals, preferences, and activity information to provide personalized workout recommendations.

AI-generated recommendations are intended for general fitness guidance and should not be considered professional medical advice.

## 5. Nutrition and Image Data

Users may provide meal information or images when using nutrition tracking features.

Such information is used only to support nutrition-related functionality and improve the user's tracking experience.

## 6. Community Features

FitFlow provides community features that allow users to interact with other users.

Users should avoid sharing sensitive personal information in public or community areas of the application.

## 7. Data Security

FitFlow is designed to use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect user information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

## 8. User Rights

Users may have the right to:

- Access their personal information.
- Request correction of inaccurate information.
- Request deletion of their information.
- Manage privacy preferences.
- Withdraw consent where applicable.

## 9. GDPR and Privacy Considerations

FitFlow aims to follow applicable privacy principles, including transparency, data minimization, purpose limitation, and user control.

Where applicable, user information should be handled in accordance with relevant data protection requirements such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## 10. Health Information Disclaimer

FitFlow is a fitness and wellness application and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition.

Users should consult an appropriate healthcare professional before making significant changes to their exercise or nutrition routines.

## 11. Children's Privacy

FitFlow is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent where required by applicable law.

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when FitFlow features, data practices, or legal requirements change.

Any important changes should be communicated through the application or associated release information.

## 13. Contact and Support

For privacy-related questions or support regarding FitFlow, users may contact the FitFlow development team.
